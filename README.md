# Wortschatz — Moin, Deutsch!

A German flashcard app: single page, no build step, installable on your phone, works offline.



## Use it everywhere

- **Install as an app**: the site is a PWA — on Android/Chrome use *Add to Home Screen / Install*,
  on iOS Safari use *Share → Add to Home Screen*. It launches standalone with its own icon.
- **Offline**: a service worker caches the app; your words are stored in `localStorage` on the device.
- **Responsive**: built for phones first (320 px and up, notched screens, landscape) and laid out properly on tablets and laptops — on screens ≥ 1000 px the menu moves into a single top bar and the study screen gets a side panel (today's goal, your round plan, quick practice) next to a bigger flashcard.

## Layout

Five pills at the top, one job each — no endless scrolling page:

- **Lernen** — just the card. Two small chips above it show your 🔥 streak and today's goal; the third chip ("10 · Mix · 40/40") opens the **round settings** sheet (cards per round, mode, which decks). When a special round is running, a coloured banner names it and its **Beenden** button takes you back to normal learning.
- **Üben** — all ungraded practice in one place: 🔁 Wiederholung, 🧠 Übungen, 🔥 Schwere Wörter, 🏋️ Training and 📌 Meine Auswahl as tiles with live counts, plus an amber banner when a deck is due for a refresh (a dot on the pill tells you from anywhere).
- **Decks** — every deck as a tile with an on/off switch, emoji, progress bar and word count, grouped and foldable, with an overall summary on top.
- **Wörter** — search, a deck dropdown, state filters and the word details; „Auswählen“ starts a hand-picked round or sends the words to Training.
- **Neu** — add or edit a word (with AI fill-in).

The account (cloud chip) and the app language (globe) sit in the top-right corner.

> Words are stored in the browser (localStorage) and can additionally sync between devices with an account — see below.

## Accounts & sync

The app has built-in accounts ("Konto & Sync" via the cloud button in the header): register once, log in on each device, and your words plus the full learning progress sync automatically. The deck is pulled when the app opens and whenever you come back to it, and pushed a few seconds after you stop answering (one upload per pause, not one per card).

Studying on two devices never loses progress: every saved deck has a revision number, and when another device saved in between, the server refuses the stale upload and the app merges both versions card by card — the schedule from the latest real grading, practice stats from the latest change, the text from the latest edit, and deletions win over older edits. Two open tabs merge the same way. Failed uploads retry with backoff, and logging out first uploads anything unsaved.

You stay logged in via a secure (`HttpOnly`) session cookie that renews itself for 90 days on every visit — even if the browser clears local storage, the app logs itself back in and restores the deck from the server.

Both server endpoints only accept JSON from the app's own origin. Logins are rate-limited per IP and per account. The AI endpoint only runs the app's three fixed tasks (article, auto-fill, translation), with the prompts, model and limits set on the server, so the API key can't be used as a general-purpose proxy.

## Features

- **Spaced repetition (SM-2)** — the Anki-style algorithm: four grades (Nochmal / Schwer / Gut / Leicht) with the projected interval shown on each button, learning steps for new cards (1/10 min), growing review intervals (interval × per-card ease factor, with fuzz), and lapse handling (failed reviews drop into relearning with a reduced interval and ease). Scheduling is day-based like Anki: day intervals come due at 03:00 of the target day, so a card graded in the evening is ready the next morning; cards in learning steps stay in the due count and rounds via a 20-minute learn-ahead window, and re-queued cards (learning steps, failures, presented new words) reappear a few cards later in the round rather than at its end. Decks from the previous Leitner version migrate automatically with progress preserved
- **Decks** — the **Decks** tab shows every deck as a tile: emoji, name, translated subtitle, an on/off switch, a progress bar (green = learned & in repetition, amber = learning, grey = untouched), its status ("16 neu", "✓ gelernt" or "⏰ vor 4 Tagen gelernt") and its word count, which opens the deck's words in Wörter. Tiles are grouped (Themen & Grammatik, Sätze & Floskeln, Grundwortschatz, Länder); each group folds (the app remembers which ones you keep open) and has its own „Alle an/aus“, and a summary on top counts active decks, learned / learning / new / due words. Grammar decks get a violet tint, the literary 🥀 Melancholie deck a rose one. Switching decks never reshuffles a round you are in the middle of — the next round is dealt with the new decks when you go back to Lernen. With every deck switched off, the counters turn red and Lernen offers a one-tap „Alle Decks anschalten“
- **Rounds ("Pro Runde")** — practice in chunks of 5 / 10 / 15 / 25 cards instead of everything at once (set it in the round settings sheet, opened from the chip above the card or from Decks); the done screen shows how many cards are still due and starts the next chunk
- **Round banner** — whenever a special round runs (Wiederholung, Übungen, Training, a selection, a deck refresh, studying ahead …) a coloured banner above the card names it and says whether it counts for your schedule („Ohne Wertung“ / „Mit Wertung“); its **Beenden** button takes you straight back to your normal learning round
- **Smart round composition ("Modus")** — *which* cards enter a round is dealt by rules, not chance: overdue reviews first, then cards in learning, then new words in book order; words you finished this sitting don't repeat in the next rounds; the counter shows the mix ("3 fällig + 7 neu"); switch the mode to Mix / Neue / Fällige. But *the order you face them in* is shuffled fresh on every deal — including selections, reviews and round repeats — so you can't anticipate the next card from its position and memorize the sequence instead of the words
- **Informative round progress** — the slim bar above the card now tells the whole story of the round: a stats line shows **cards done and cards left** on one side ("7 geschafft · 16 übrig" — "left" honestly includes retests that failed or presented cards still owe) and **gewusst · wiederholt · percent** on the other, while the bar itself is segmented: green = answered correctly, amber = missed, pale green = new words seen but not yet quizzed, grey = still ahead; hover for the full breakdown
- **Learn state for new words** — cards you have never studied are presented first (word, translation, example and note all visible) before they get quizzed later in the same round; "Kenn ich schon" skips a known word straight to a 4-day interval
- **Repeat & drill** — "Diese Runde wiederholen" runs the same words again as an ungraded practice round (your schedule stays untouched); in the word list, "Auswählen" lets you hand-pick any words and start them as a real learning round or a drill — and the selection respects your "Pro Runde" size: pick 40 words and study them as chunks of 5/10/15/25, with a "Weiter mit Auswahl (n übrig)" button chaining through your whole pick; changing Pro Runde or Modus mid-selection simply re-chunks the remaining selected words instead of dropping you back into the full deck (the same applies inside review rounds)
- **Daily review across all decks (🔁 Wiederholung)** — one tap on its tile in **Üben** drills *every word you have ever studied*, no matter which deck it came from and whether that deck is currently active. The capture is guaranteed: the moment a card is graded or even just shown as a new-word presentation, it's stamped with a study date — so what you did yesterday across three different decks is 100% in today's review, front of the queue (the pool is ordered newest study-day first, most-urgent first within a day, and the bar shows it: "214 Wörter · 32 von heute & gestern"). Rounds are dealt in your "Pro Runde" size with a "Weiter wiederholen (n übrig)" button chaining chunks until you've been through everything, then the cycle restarts; the pool grows automatically as you learn more. It's pure extra exposure: everything runs as an ungraded practice round, so your SM-2 intervals are never distorted. Existing decks get their study dates backfilled from the scheduler on first load
- **Saved selection (📌 Meine Auswahl)** — whenever you hand-pick words with "Auswählen" and start them, that exact pick is saved as its own little deck, shown as a tile in **Üben** ("Meine Auswahl · 8 Wörter · 18.07.26"). Tap it any day to drill precisely those words again (chunked, ungraded); a new selection replaces it, and the × on the tile deletes it. Synced with your account
- **Deck repeat reminders (⏰)** — the app notices when a deck you once studied has been resting: three days after a deck's last real study session an amber banner appears at the top of **Üben** (and a dot lights up on the Üben pill) — "Präpositionen · vor 2 Wochen gelernt · 14 Wörter · Wiederholen" — always naming the most-neglected deck first (with a "+n" hint when more are waiting). One tap drills exactly that deck's studied words in Pro-Runde chunks (ungraded, schedule untouched); afterwards the next stale deck's reminder surfaces. The deck tiles join in: a stale deck is tinted amber in **Decks** with "⏰ vor 2 Wochen gelernt" as its status line. Repeating (or studying the deck again) resets its clock, and the reminder state syncs with your account
- **Difficult words (🔥 Schwere Wörter)** — the app watches which words you keep missing: every "Nochmal / didn't know" — in real rounds *and* in practice rounds — counts, and a word becomes "difficult" once you've missed it 3 times, forgotten it twice after it was learned (lapses), or the scheduler keeps rating it hard (low ease). Those words form their own virtual deck as a tile in **Üben** ("12 Wörter · oft nicht gewusst") — one tap drills exactly them, hardest first, in Pro-Runde chunks, ungraded so the schedule stays clean. Answer a difficult word correctly 4 times in a row and it graduates off the list — miss it once more and it's back. You don't have to wait for the automatic rule either: every study card carries a small 🔥 pin in its corner — tap it the moment a word feels hard and it goes straight onto the list (tap the lit pin to take it off again, even for auto-detected words); the same toggle sits in the word-detail view. The word list gets a matching "Schwere" filter, and each difficult word shows a 🔥 "schwer zu merken" badge with its miss count in the detail view; existing decks derive the counts from their lapse history
- **Exercises (🧠 Übungen)** — flashcards show you the answer, exercises make you *choose* it: a tile in **Üben** runs active-recall drills over every word you have ever studied (fed by the same guaranteed capture as the Wiederholung pool). Each word becomes one of four question types, picked to fit it: *Was bedeutet das?* (pick the right translation out of 4), *Welches Wort ist das?* (reverse — see the meaning, pick the German word), *Der, die oder das?* (article drill for nouns) and *Welches Wort fehlt?* (the word blanked out of its own example sentence). Distractors are drawn from the same deck and the same word class first (verbs against verbs, nouns against nouns), so the wrong options are plausible, not random noise; a correct pick flashes green, a wrong one turns red, reveals the answer, requeues the word a few cards later in the round *and* counts toward the 🔥 difficult-words detector — so words you keep fumbling in exercises surface in Schwere Wörter automatically. And every answer teaches more than one word: the moment you pick, *all* options reveal their meaning in small print (the German word under each translation, the translation under each German word), the cloze sentence fills in its blank with the word in bold, and the article drill shows what the noun means — three extra words of passive exposure per question, at a glimpse. Cloze questions work the sentence even harder: its translation sits right under the gap (so filling it is real recall, not grammar guessing), and words you've *already studied* that naturally appear in the sentence are marked with a dotted underline — their meanings join the reveal after you answer ("Auch im Satz: auf — on, onto"), and sentences carrying your studied words are picked for cloze about twice as often, so every gap-fill quietly re-exposes old vocabulary in fresh context. Nothing advances on its own: the reveals stay up as long as you want, and you move on yourself with Weiter (or Space / →). Keys 1–4 answer from the keyboard, rounds are dealt in your Pro-Runde chunks with a "Weiter üben (n übrig)" chain, and like every practice mode it never touches your SM-2 schedule
- **Training (🏋️) — practise exactly the words you choose** — a dedicated trainer, separate from the mixed Übungen, for *any* words: nouns, verbs, adjectives, phrases. Open it from its tile in **Üben**, or select words in the word list ("Auswählen") and tap **🏋️ Trainieren** in the selection bar — every selected word comes along. **Step 1 — choose words:** all words are listed A–Z as a checklist (one column on phones, a grid on bigger screens, drawn in chunks so even 1,400 words open instantly); tick precisely the ones you want (say *der Kopf, gehen, klein*) and they collect in a **✋ Deine Auswahl** tray above the list, each with an × to drop it and a „Leeren“ button to start over. Search narrows the list by German word or meaning, the deck chips narrow it to one deck, and „Alle 28 wählen / abwählen“ takes or drops everything currently shown; quick picks (Gelernte / Schwere) work within the filter; the button at the bottom moves on („Weiter mit 3 Wörtern“). **Step 2 — choose how:** 🎲 **Gemischt** (everything that fits each word, shuffled), 💬 **Bedeutung** (word ↔ translation, distractors of the same word class), ✍️ **Lückentext** (the word blanked out of its sentence with the translation underneath; verbs are found in their *conjugated* form — „Der Zug _____ um acht Uhr ___.“ — and the answer fills in „fährt … ab“), ⌨️ **Schreiben** (type the German from its meaning: nouns are expected *with* their article — „der Kopf“ is right, „Kopf“ counts as „fast richtig“ and „die Kopf“ as a miss; Enter checks, Enter again moves on, ä/ö/ü/ß buttons, ae/oe/ue/ss accepted, one-letter typos forgiven), 🏷️ **Artikel** (der/die/das) or 🃏 **Karteikarten** (ungraded flashcards). A round always includes *every* word you picked: when a word doesn't fit the chosen exercise (no example sentence for Lückentext, not a noun for Artikel), it gets a meaning question instead, and a note under the tiles says how many words that affects. Rounds run in Pro-Runde chunks with a „Weiter trainieren (n übrig)“ chain, and „Andere Übung wählen“ jumps straight back to step 2 with the same words for a second drill. After every answer the key facts are revealed — a verb's forms (er fährt ab · fuhr ab · ist abgefahren), a noun's plural. Like all practice it never touches your SM-2 schedule, but every word you train is captured into the daily 🔁 Wiederholung pool and misses feed 🔥 Schwere Wörter; your selection and favourite exercise are saved and synced. To make gap-fills work for the core verbs, the 95 everyday verbs of the Grundwortschatz (plus *schnacken*) — which had no example sentences — now come with a natural sentence, its translation and their key forms as the tip; existing decks receive them automatically without touching anything you edited yourself
- **Unlimited words & own decks** — add your own words in the **Neu** tab, and create your own decks with the dashed **+ Neues Deck** tile in Decks (empty decks persist, sync with your account, and can be removed again via their ×)
- **AI assist** — type just the German word and the AI fills in the article, translation, example sentence (with English translation) and a grammar note
- **App language (🌐, top right)** — not every German learner knows English or German: the whole interface (tabs, buttons, states, toasts, forms, deck subtitles — over 330 strings) switches between 17 languages: Deutsch, English, Türkçe, Azərbaycanca, Русский, Español, Français, Italiano, Português, Polski, Українська, Nederlands, Ελληνικά, Čeština, Română, Magyar and Svenska. For non-English/German languages the word meanings and example translations are also localized: generated in one batched AI call per round via `api/ai.js` and cached on the device (falls back to English without the AI server). The choice syncs with your account
- **Example translations** — cards with an example sentence show its English translation right below it
- **Browse decks & word details** — the word list filters by deck (a compact deck dropdown) *and by learning state* (Alle / Neue / Am Lernen / Gelernt / Fällige / **Heute & Gestern** — everything you studied today or yesterday), combined with search; every row shows the date the word was added, and the expanded detail shows both "Hinzugefügt" and "Zuletzt gelernt" dates — so you can pull up exactly the words you did on a given day in a given deck and hand-pick them via "Auswählen" to learn or drill again; when a deck is selected, a coverage header shows its total, progress bar and clickable state counts — so you can verify at a glance that every word of a deck has been covered (the word count on every deck tile in Decks jumps straight there). Tapping a word unfolds its full record: learning status with next review, color-coded article, example sentence with translation, tip and deck — plus controls to reset it to new, mark it as learned, or edit it
- **Streak & daily goal** — two chips above the card (on laptops the „Heute“ panel) show your 🔥 day streak (any day with at least one graded card keeps it alive), a daily-goal ring (tap to cycle 10/20/30/50 cards) with a confetti moment when you reach it, and your 🏆 record streak; all of it syncs with your account

### Notes on the *Sätze & Floskeln* cards

Each card's note keeps the extra context from the book:

- **hören** — a phrase the street says *to you* (cashier, conductor, loudspeaker)
- **sagen** — your line
- no tag — flows both ways
- **Norddeutsch** — Bremen/Hamburg specials

## Project structure

```
index.html            the whole app (UI, styles, data packs, logic)
api/ai.js             Vercel serverless proxy for the AI features
api/sync.js           Vercel serverless accounts + deck sync (Upstash Redis)
manifest.webmanifest  PWA manifest
sw.js                 service worker (offline cache)
icons/                favicon + home-screen icons
```

Study keyboard shortcuts: `Space` flips the card, `1`–`4` = Nochmal / Schwer / Gut / Leicht, `←` = Nochmal, `→` = Gut; in exercises `1`–`4` pick an answer and `Space` / `→` / `Enter` moves on; `Esc` closes the round settings.
